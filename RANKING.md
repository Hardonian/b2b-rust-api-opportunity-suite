## Ranking rationale

| Rank | Repo | Why first |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | trade-compliance-api | Clear API wedge, high regulatory churn, and immediate willingness to pay from importers, brokers, and cross-border commerce stacks. |
| 2 | utility-bill-normalization-api | Boring but sticky infrastructure: messy source data, recurring normalization debt, and strong CRE/energy/lender demand. |
| 3 | recall-intelligence-api | Fragmented public recall data with direct catalog and product survival impact, creating urgency for distributors and retailers. |
| 4 | warranty-infrastructure-api | Strong ROI potential, but heavier enterprise integration and supplier coordination. |
| 5 | construction-change-order-api | Large market and high pain, heavier workflow complexity and incumbent friction create longer sales cycles. |
